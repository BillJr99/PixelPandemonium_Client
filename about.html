Welcome to Pixel Pandemonium!

Pixel Pandemonium is an all-online version of Post-It Pandemonium, a hands-on CS activity in which students learn how images are represented on a computer and construct a large version of an image one pixel tile at a time.

Teachers create an activity instance, share the student URL or QR code, and watch the full image appear in the replay view.
